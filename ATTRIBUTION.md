# Flag artwork attribution

ChronoFlags stores local copies of flag artwork so the installed app can work offline. The artwork is not generated and is not covered by the app code's license. Each catalog entry links to its source record from the details panel.

Official insignia and sensitive symbols may be regulated independently of copyright. Check the law that applies where you live and how you intend to use an image.
